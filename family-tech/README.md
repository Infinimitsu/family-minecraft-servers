# Family Tech

Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge. A tech-and-magic world built so that exploring is
exciting and building big machines is the main event. The full mod list is in
[MODS.md](MODS.md).

## What you can build

- **Tech:** Create (contraptions, trains, brass), Mekanism (machines, power,
  generators, tools), Refined Storage, Compact Machines, CC: Tweaked computers.
- **Magic:** Ars Nouveau (spell crafting), Occultism (spirits and rituals), EvilCraft
  (blood magic).
- **Shortcuts:** ProjectE (turn items into EMC and back) and Mystical Agriculture
  (grow resources as crops).
- **Money:** Numismatics adds coins, shops and bank accounts on top of Create.
- **Building:** Chipped, Macaw's furniture/roofs/doors/windows/bridges, Handcrafted,
  Supplementaries, FramedBlocks, Building Wands.
- **Storage and travel:** Sophisticated Backpacks, Iron Chests, EnderChests,
  EnderPack, Waystones.
- **Farming and food:** Let's Do Vinery (vineyards) and Camping (tents, sleeping bags).

## What makes the world different

- **Roads between towns.** The Routes mod builds named roads with signposts,
  bridges, tunnels and rest stops between the villages and towns, and you spawn in a
  town that's already on the network. Towns and roads show up on Xaero's maps.
- **Bigger biomes.** Tectonic terrain with Oh The Biomes We've Gone, tuned so each
  biome covers about twice the vanilla area.
- **Lots to find.** Over 30 structure mods: Towns and Towers, Repurposed Structures,
  When Dungeons Arise, Dungeons and Taverns, Explorify, YUNG's, Moog's, Create: Let The
  Adventure Begin, Dungeon Crawl, Yggdrasil, Luki's Grand Capitals and more.
- **A living world.** Naturalist, Spawn, Critters and Companions, Friends & Foes, Ecologics,
  ducks, frog villages (Ribbits), Creeper and Enderman variants, wall-crawling spiders, new
  cave biomes (Galosphere), a bigger Deep Dark (Deeper and Darker), Mowzie's Mobs, and boss
  fights from L_Ender's Cataclysm, Legendary Monsters, Mutant Monsters and The Graveyard.
  Villages get guards (Guard Villagers) and new professions (Villagers Plus).
- **Protected towns.** Villages and towns can't be broken or built on (crops, flowers and
  torches excepted), and their villagers can't be hurt, so they stay intact for everyone.
  Claim your own base with Open Parties and Claims.
- **Generous loot.** Every chest from every mod gets bonus loot by how dangerous the
  place is:
  - Village houses and camps: food, iron, gold, emeralds, diamonds, starter mod materials.
  - Dungeons, temples, ruins and mineshafts: diamonds, ancient debris, netherite scrap,
    upgrade templates, strong enchanted books.
  - Treasure rooms and boss vaults: netherite ingots, upgrade templates, enchanted
    diamond and netherite gear, totems, enchanted golden apples.
  - Stacks in chests are doubled, mobs drop twice as much (plus the odd emerald or
    diamond), fishing is mostly treasure, and brushing suspicious sand or gravel often
    turns up gems.
- **Your own loot.** With Lootr, each player gets their own copy of every
  generated chest, so nobody finds one already emptied, and chests refill after a
  few days. Chests you place yourself work normally.
- **Shiny caves.** Big caves and caverns have ore patches right on their walls, floors
  and ceilings, including Create and Mekanism ores. There are diamond and emerald
  geodes to stumble on, more amethyst geodes, and about three times the ancient debris
  in the Nether.

## Looks and sound

Fresh Animations (with its official extensions and player animations) is on by default:
mobs and players blink, breathe and move naturally. Also Entity Texture/Model Features,
Not Enough Animations, 3D armor and arrows, Wavey Capes, 3D Skin Layers, Particle Rain,
Subtle Effects and AmbientSounds, plus optional shader packs.

## Also on the server

Simple Voice Chat (proximity voice), Vivecraft (play in VR or on a normal screen),
Distant Horizons (see far beyond the normal view distance), Xaero's Minimap and
World Map, Open Parties and Claims (protect your base), Gravestone (keep your items
when you die), and JEI and Jade for looking things up.

## Server-side tuning

[`server/`](server/) holds the scripts and datapacks the server runs:
- `kubejs/server_scripts/family_loot.js`: the loot tiers (KubeJS + LootJS).
- `kubejs/server_scripts/family_protection.js`: town protection.
- `kubejs/server_scripts/family_switches.js`: switch a mod's creatures off or on.
- `datapacks/family_worldgen/`: cave ores, gem geodes and ancient debris
  (generated by `make_worldgen.py`).
- `datapacks/family_fixes/`: small fixes for other mods' data.
