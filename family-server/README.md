# Family Server

Minecraft 1.21.4 on Fabric. Our long-running family survival world. It was
restored from backups and from the terrain players' Distant Horizons caches had saved,
so years of builds are still there. The mods stay light and close to vanilla. The
full list is in [MODS.md](MODS.md).

- **Distant Horizons:** see the whole world far beyond the normal view distance.
- **Simple Voice Chat:** proximity voice chat.
- **Vivecraft:** join in VR or on a normal screen.
- **Xaero's Minimap and World Map.**
- **Performance:** Lithium, FerriteCore, plus Sodium and Iris on clients.
- **Optional shaders:** BSL, Bliss, Complementary (Reimagined and Unbound), Photon.
  Pick any on first install.
